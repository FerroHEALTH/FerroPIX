- The operator console signs in and keeps its sessions through `ferrohealth-session` 0.1.0, the FerroHEALTH console sign-in crate, in place of its own copy. `[oidc]` now needs only `issuer`, `client_id` and `redirect_uri`: the provider's endpoints are discovered when the console starts, and `authorization_endpoint`, `token_endpoint`, `jwks_uri`, `end_session_endpoint` and the new `revocation_endpoint` each take the place of the discovered one; `ca_file` and `timeout_s` (default 10) are new. `[session]` gains `store` (`memory`, the default, or `sealed` with `key` or `key_file`), and refuses an `absolute_timeout_s` shorter than `idle_timeout_s`. `config check` calls no provider: a section that leaves the endpoints to discovery is read when the console starts. The routes `/login`, `/auth/callback`, `/logout` and `/session` keep their paths; a sign-out now revokes the session's token when the provider has a revocation endpoint, and a console with no provider ends the session and lands on the signed-out page in place of answering `503`. A page whose session lives on but whose access token the provider could not renew sends the steward to the new `/sign-in/unrenewed`, which says so and leads back to the page.
