## The crates

`ihe-iti` and `nl-generic-functions` each ship the licence in their package.
A product that depends on them takes them under the same terms.

## Vendored material

The specification texts under `docs/specs/` keep their upstream terms, each
recorded in the `PROVENANCE.md` beside the text. A text whose licence keeps
it out of a public repository is fetched at build time and never committed.

## Contributions

A contribution is licensed under the same terms, and grants the Licensor the
relicensing right in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/FerroHEALTH/FerroPIX/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#licensing-of-contributions).
The pull request's checkbox records it. There is no separate agreement to
sign.
