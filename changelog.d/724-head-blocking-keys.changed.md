- The two text blocking keys live beside each record's head (`pix.record_block`), written by the statement that moves the head version, instead of on every version. A feed's blocking read and an ITI-119 match touch only the current records that carry the key, so their cost no longer grows with each record's history. The migration fills the keys of every head in batches of 1,000 and drops the version columns and their indexes.
