- Breaking for operators and crate users: FerroPIX moves to `ferrohealth-smart` 0.32 and `ferrohealth-session` 0.27. Issuers are named tables, `[auth.issuer.<name>]` in place of `[[auth.issuer]]`; an issuer names its preset as `preset` (the `nl` preset is gone) and sets its profile keys (`first_party_clients`, `delegates`, `token_types`, `required_claims`, `client_claims` and the rest) under `[auth.issuer.<name>.profile]`; its `scopeless_subjects`, `federation` and `attributes` sit under it in place of the section-level `scopeless_subjects`, `[[auth.federation]]` and `[[auth.attributes]]`; and `[[trust.issuer]] issuer` and `[auth] described_issuer` name an issuer by its table's name. An issuer that sets no `audience` takes the FHIR base, `{public_base_url}/fhir`. Every `401` and `403` challenge carries the refusal's reason code as `error_description` (RFC 6750 §3), `scope-not-granted` for a token without the route's scope. The quickstart, compose file, Helm chart (`config.auth.issuer` is a map by name) and book show the new shape. `ihe-iti` 0.17.0 requires `ferrohealth-smart` 0.32, and `nl-generic-functions` 0.17.0 requires `ferrohealth-smart` 0.32 and `ihe-iti` 0.17: their public API carries smart's outbound client and endpoint table, so a consumer moves to the same lines. The server takes `ferrobridge-term` and `ferrobridge-hl7v2` on smart 0.32, so one smart links.
