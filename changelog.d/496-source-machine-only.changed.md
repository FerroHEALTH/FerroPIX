- A Patient Identity Source is admitted only with a machine's token, one the issuer's profile classifies as app-only from the client credentials grant. A person's token through the Source's client, a first-party client listed in `[[trust.issuer]] clients`, is answered `403` with a `forbidden` issue whose diagnostics say a Source sends a client credentials token, on ITI-104 and on the ITI-93 feed. A Source that fed with a person's token requests a client credentials token for its `source_client_id`.
