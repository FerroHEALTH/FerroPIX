- `ihe-iti` 0.0.70 and `nl-generic-functions` 0.0.37 write each shape the PDQm, PIXm, mCSD, PMIR, XCPD, NVI and Mitz clients share once. `authorizer::send` is generic over the client's error, which implements the new `exchange::Unanswered` and `authorizer::Unauthenticated`, and `authorizer::Unsent` is gone; `exchange::read` reads an answer to a bound, and `soap::post`, with the new `soap::Refusal`, sends a SOAP 1.2 message over HTTP, so the `soap` feature now compiles `reqwest`. The PDQm and PIXm `Malformation` hold `NotFhirJson`, `TooLarge`, `NotJson`, `NotAResource` and `Decode` in the new `outcome::Unreadable`, matched as `Malformation::Unreadable(Unreadable::NotFhirJson)`. `mcsd::directory::DirectoryEntry<Organization>` and `DirectoryEntry<Endpoint>`, over the sealed `DirectoryResource`, replace `DirectoryOrganization` and `DirectoryEndpoint`. `pixm::server::feed::Revision::into_patient` and `server::answer::Answer::into_resource`, which nothing called, are removed. Every request and answer on the wire is unchanged.
