- `ihe-iti` 0.0.68 reads and writes the SOAP 1.2 envelope of ITI TF-2 Appendix V once, in the new `soap` feature: `soap::read` and `soap::answer` read an envelope through a `soap::Visitor`, `soap::write` writes one, and `soap::FaultCode`, `soap::Malformed` and the `ENVELOPE`, `ADDRESSING`, `ANONYMOUS` and `MEDIA_TYPE` constants are its own. `xcpd` reads and writes through it. `nl-generic-functions` 0.0.35's `mitz` feature now depends on `ihe-iti` with `soap` and reads the Mitz answer's envelope through it. A product changes `xcpd::error::FaultCode` and `mitz::error::FaultCode` to `ihe_iti::soap::FaultCode` (Mitz now names `VersionMismatch`, `MustUnderstand` and `DataEncodingUnknown`, which it reported as another code), and the `NotSoap`, `NotXml`, `DocumentType`, `TooDeep` and `NotAnEnvelope` variants of `xcpd::error::Malformation` and `mitz::error::Malformation` to `Malformation::Envelope(soap::Malformed::…)`. Every request, answer and fault on the wire is unchanged.
