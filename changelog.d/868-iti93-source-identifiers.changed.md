- The ITI-93 message of a merge names the deprecated Patient by the identifiers its records held before the merge, in each domain's system, beside its master identifier (PMIR 1.6.0 §2:3.93.4.1.2.4, the Merged Patient Profile leaves `Patient.identifier` at 0..*), and the delete entry of a deleted master identity carries the deleted Patient with the identifiers it held (the History Bundle Profile's `PMIREntryDelete.resource`, 0..1). A Patient Identity Consumer that keys its state by source identifier learns from the message which identifiers the change concerns. In ihe-iti 0.29.0, `pmir::server::notification::Notice::Deleted` carries that `Patient` in place of its id.
