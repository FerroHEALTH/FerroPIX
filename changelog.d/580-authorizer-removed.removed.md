- Breaking for a crate user: `ihe-iti` 0.2.0 drops the `authorizer` and `smart` features with the `ihe_iti::authorizer` module (`Authorizer`, `Authorizable` and `with_authorizer`, `AuthorizerError`, `Retry`, `send`, and `smart::TokenAuthorizer`), and `nl-generic-functions` 0.2.0 drops `NviClient::with_authorizer` and `mitz::error::ClientError`. The credential an authorizer made comes from the endpoint table of the client's `OutboundClient`; the send every client goes through is the new `outbound` feature's `ihe_iti::outbound::send`.
