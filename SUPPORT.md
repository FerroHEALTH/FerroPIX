# Getting help

## I have a question

Start at the [README](README.md) and the pin matrix,
[`docs/VERSIONS.md`](docs/VERSIONS.md). If those do not answer it, open an
issue through the
[issue chooser](https://github.com/FerroHEALTH/FerroPIX/issues/new/choose).

There is no commercial support offering and no service-level agreement. A
commercial licence or any other business question goes to Cadasto B.V., the
Licensor: [info@cadasto.com](mailto:info@cadasto.com) or
<https://www.cadasto.com/contact/>.

## I found a defect

[Open an issue](https://github.com/FerroHEALTH/FerroPIX/issues/new/choose)
when a transaction answers differently from what the IHE specification
names, an identifier is linked or resolved wrongly, or identifying data
reaches a log line. The status and the `OperationOutcome` or acknowledgment
help; never paste patient data or a real identifier.

## I found a vulnerability

Do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
